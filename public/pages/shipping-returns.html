UK Standard Orders

Final Sale Policy
All sales are final once payment is received. No refunds are provided.
Faulty Items
Replacements or exchanges are only granted if the item was sent faulty and remains completely unused and unworn.
Style Changes
You can request a style change within 24 hours of payment. Any price difference must be settled before processing.
Shipping Cost & Days
Standard Service deliveries are free of charge. Deliveries are made Monday to Saturday, excluding Sundays and public holidays.
Ready to Ship (In-Stock Items)
Subject to a 1–3 working days processing time before shipment. Once shipped, UK delivery takes 2–5 working days.
Standard Inventory
Subject to a 3–5 working days standard processing and quality check window before dispatch. Once shipped, delivery takes an estimated 5–10 working days.
Tracking & Signatures
You will receive a tracking email once dispatched. Wigs and bundles require a signature upon delivery. If you are not home, the courier will leave a delivery note.

International Orders

Final Sale Policy
All sales are final once payment is received. No refunds are provided.
Style Changes
Allowed within 24 hours of payment. Any price difference will be transferred if necessary.
Shipping Cost & Days
DHL Express deliveries cost £28. Deliveries run Monday to Friday, excluding weekends and public holidays.
Standard & Custom International Shipping
Orders are subject to a 3–5 working days processing window, followed by an estimated 7–14 working days for international transit and customs clearance.
Customs & Duties
You will receive a dispatch email when your order ships. The recipient is entirely responsible for all customs charges and import duties. Emm Luxury Hair is not liable for these fees.

Custom Units & Liability Terms

Final Approvals
Before your custom unit is dispatched, we may send a final preview, invoice confirmation, or tracking information via email or WhatsApp. Once you confirm, no changes, refunds, or cancellations are permitted.
No-Cancellation Policy
Because custom units are unique and tailored to your specifications, they cannot be resold. Therefore, they fall under a strict no-cancellation, no-refund policy.
Transfer of Liability
Once the courier marks the parcel as delivered and signed for, liability transfers completely to the customer.
Lost or Stolen Packages
Emm Luxury Hair is not responsible for any loss, theft, or misplacement once the courier confirms delivery. All units are shipped via tracked delivery — you must ensure someone is available to receive the package at your shipping address.
