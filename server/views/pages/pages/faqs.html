FAQs — Frequently Asked Questions

Hair Quality & Sourcing

What makes EMM LUXURY Hair different from standard hair extensions?
Our hair is 100% ethically sourced, premium virgin hair with intact, aligned cuticles. It undergoes zero harsh chemical processing, ensuring natural shine, longevity, and a tangle-free experience that mimics natural hair.
What is the difference between Single Donor and Multiple Donor hair?
Single donor hair comes from one individual, meaning the texture, bleachability, and cuticle direction are perfectly uniform. This represents the highest tier of luxury hair available.
Can EMM LUXURY Hair be bleached, dyed, and heat-styled?
Yes. Because our hair is completely natural and unprocessed, it can be bleached up to a level 10 (lightest blonde) and styled with hot tools just like your own hair. We always recommend professional colouring to maintain hair integrity.

Sizing, Selection & Fit

How do I choose the correct cap size for my glueless wig?
We offer a comprehensive sizing chart on our website. Measure your head circumference, front-to-nape, and ear-to-ear using a soft tape measure to ensure a flawless, secure fit.
What density should I choose?
Density refers to the thickness of the hair. We offer 150% (natural/everyday fullness), 180% (glamorous volume), and 200%+ (maximum luxury thickness).
What is HD Lace, and why is it premium?
High-Definition (HD) lace is the thinnest, most undetectable lace on the market. It melts seamlessly into all skin tones without heavy tinting, offering an incredibly realistic hairline.

Longevity & Maintenance

How long will my EMM LUXURY Hair last?
With proper maintenance, our luxury bundles and wigs can last between 1 to 3+ years. Lace closures and frontals may need replacement periodically due to natural wear and styling.
How often should I wash my luxury wig or installations?
We recommend washing your hair every 10 to 14 days, or after about 15–20 wears, to prevent product buildup and keep the hair moving naturally.
Can I swim or exercise in my luxury hair?
While you can, we advise avoiding regular exposure to pool chlorine and ocean saltwater. If you do swim, protect the hair in a high bun or braid, and wash it immediately afterward using moisturizing products.
